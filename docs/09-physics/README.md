# 09: The physics underneath

> **Status: planned.** This module is scoped but not yet written. The scope
> below is the commitment; it is here so you can see where the curriculum is
> going and tell us if the plan is wrong.

The mechanics that has been doing the work all along, made explicit.

## Planned lessons

- Newton's law of gravitation, and where the inverse square comes from
- Why orbits are conic sections
- Kepler's three laws, derived rather than asserted
- Angular momentum and why the Moon is receding
- Energy, escape velocity, and why orbits are hard to change

## Prerequisites

- Module 03
- comfort with algebra; calculus optional

## Intended approach

Mostly prose and small numerical experiments. Some derivations at Advanced layer, all results usable without them.
