# 11: Rocketry

> **Status: planned.** This module is scoped but not yet written. The scope
> below is the commitment; it is here so you can see where the curriculum is
> going and tell us if the plan is wrong.

Why getting to orbit is hard, quantitatively.

## Planned lessons

- The rocket equation, derived from conservation of momentum
- Why staging exists
- Delta-v budgets for real destinations
- Gravity losses and why rockets turn sideways early
- Why the Moon is easier to reach than it looks, and Mars harder

## Prerequisites

- Module 10

## Intended approach

Prose plus a delta-v calculator. Nothing here needs new astronomy.
