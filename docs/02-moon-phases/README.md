# 02: Moon phases

Starts with a question you can answer by looking up: **what phase is the Moon
in right now?** Ends with you having built the calculation yourself and
understanding why two reasonable models disagree.

## Lessons

| # | Lesson | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Go and look](go-and-look.md) | An observation log, started today |
| 2 | [Why phases happen](why-phases-happen.md) | The geometry, with a lamp and a ball |

## Prerequisites

[Module 01](../01-time-and-place/), or at least a working install and a saved
location.

## The observation gate

Lesson 1 asks you to start a log and add to it over about a month. **Start it
now** and keep reading; do not wait. The calculation lessons are much more
interesting when you have your own data to check them against, and a lunar
cycle takes as long as it takes.

Cloudy for a week? Note the gap and carry on. Missing data is data.

## What you will be able to do

- Say what phase the Moon is in without looking it up
- Predict where and when to see it
- Compute the phase for any date, two different ways
- Explain why the simple model drifts, and by how much
- Explain the 29.5 versus 27.3 day puzzle from first principles
